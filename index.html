Doddily finds baby, toddler and kids (up to 8yrs) classes, groups, soft play and parks near you, and lays them out like a timetable. No more digging through a dozen websites.

This is the app itself. Tap around: it shows what’s on near E8 1EA.

Set it up once, then just open it.

  1. Add your postcode

    Or use where you are, and choose how far you’re happy to walk or drive.

  2. Add your children

    A name and an age group each, from newborns to pre-school. Add as many children as you like, and classes they’re too young or too old for stay out of the way.

  3. Pick a day and go

    Sessions in time order with all the information you’ll need to go. Directions, website, contact info, price, opening hours, ages and how to book! You can even add them to your calendar/reminder and save your favourites.

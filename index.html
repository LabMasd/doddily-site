Set it up once, then just open it.

Doddily turns scattered timetables into one list for the day you pick, starting with what’s nearest and soonest.

  1. Add your postcode

    Or use where you are, and choose how far you’re happy to walk or drive.

  2. Add your children

    A name and birth month each. Classes they’re too young or too old for stay out of the way.

  3. Pick a day and go

    Sessions in time order with walking time, then places you can go any time.

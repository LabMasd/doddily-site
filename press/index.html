More

Open it, add your postcode and your children’s ages, and Today shows that day’s sessions in time order with the walking time from your door. Filters for free, no booking and rainy day. Four screens: Today, Map, Saved, You.

Doddily only lists classes and places that are already published online, and every entry links back to the provider. Class providers can have their sessions listed by emailing hello@doddily.app with a link to their page.

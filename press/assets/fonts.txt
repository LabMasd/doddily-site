Doddily type

Both typefaces are free Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License).

Display: Bricolage Grotesque
- Headlines and the wordmark
- Weights used: 700 (headlines), 800 (the wordmark, big numbers)
- https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Bricolage+Grotesque

Text: Figtree
- Body copy, buttons, labels
- Weights used: 400, 500, 600
- https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Figtree

Setting
- Sentence case throughout; no all-caps.
- Headlines: letter-spacing −0.02em, line-height 1.02 to 1.1.
- Body: 17px / 1.55 on screen.

The wordmark in logos/ is already converted to outlines, so you do not need the font installed to use it.

## 1. The founder story (Victoria’s voice)

Two weeks after my second was born I spent a whole nap time trying to answer one question: what’s on near us this morning that both kids could go to?

Instagram had half of it. A Facebook group had a third. The council had a PDF from 2024. The library rhyme time wasn’t anywhere.

So I built Doddily. Open it, add your postcode and your children’s ages, and it shows today’s classes, groups and soft play near you, in time order, with the walking time from your door.

13,500 classes across the UK on day one. No account, no ads, nothing collected. It launched on the App Store this week and I still can’t quite believe I get to use it on Monday.

If you know a parent of a baby or toddler, send it their way. And if you run a class, email us a link and we’ll add it.

## 2. The product

We launched Doddily this week: baby and toddler classes near you, laid out like a timetable.

The brief we gave ourselves was one sentence. “A tired parent should know what to do this morning in under ten seconds.” Everything else followed from it.

- Four screens, and that’s the app: Today, Map, Saved, You.
- Today is a timetable, not a feed. Sessions in time order, walking time on every one.
- Add your children’s ages once and it only shows what suits them.
- Filters that matter: free, no booking, rainy day.
- One tap to directions, booking, calendar, reminder.
- No account. Nothing leaves the phone.

13,500 classes and groups, 129,000 parks and places, UK-wide, from the providers’ own published pages. iPhone now, Android next. Link in the first comment.

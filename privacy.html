Privacy

Last updated 16 September 2026

Doddily doesn’t ask you to create an account and doesn’t collect personal data.

Your location

If you choose “Use where I am”, your phone’s location is used to find activities near you. It’s only sent to look up the nearest postcode (postcodes.io) and to load listings for your area. We don’t store it.

Your postcode, children and saved places

Your postcode, distance, your name and your children’s names and birth months are saved on your phone only. So are the places you save and any reminders you set.

Calendar and reminders

Doddily only adds an event when you tap “Add to calendar” and confirm it, and it can’t read your calendar. Reminders use your phone’s own notifications.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

None.

Listings

Class and group details come from providers’ public pages, councils and libraries, and each one links back to the provider. Map and place data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Contact

Questions, or want a listing corrected or removed? Email hello@doddily.app.

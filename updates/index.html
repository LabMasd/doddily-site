Look further ahead, and further afield.

  • Plan the month. Tap the arrow under the dates to open a calendar and pick any day in the next four weeks.
  • Go further. Set your distance up to 25 miles, from the list or straight from the map.
  • Walk or drive. Nearby classes show the walk; longer trips show the drive too, and Directions opens the right kind of route.
  • Evenings look to tomorrow. Open the app after the day’s classes are done and it starts on tomorrow.
  • Clearer filters. The ones you’ve chosen come first, and one tap clears them all.
  • Search and filters on the map.
  • Bigger text fits. The app follows your iPhone’s text size.
  • Fixed: your postcode and distance now stay put in ‘You’.
